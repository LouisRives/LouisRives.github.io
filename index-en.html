Journey

I'm Louis, a French-Finnish developer, freshly graduated with a Master's in Game Technology from BUAS in Breda.

Right now I'm travelling, playing games, and coding. Not necessarily in that order. Travel is a real passion: seeing how different people live, play, and create genuinely changes how you think about everything.

On the code side, I'm working on a fast-paced arena shooter inspired by Quake and Team Fortress 2, built in Unreal Engine 5. It's my training ground for the engine, but mostly a game I actually want to play. I've also got 150h+ in GameMaker with Hyper Pulsar, a hardcore top-down shooter in the vein of Hotline Miami and Katana Zero. And I'm a big souls-like fan, which isn't unrelated to what my Master's thesis ended up being about.

What genuinely interests me is game feel. What players experience without knowing exactly why, that invisible detail that changes everything. And it connects to something few people know about me: haute couture fascinates me as much as games do. Designers like Carol Christian Poell, Yohji Yamamoto, Issey Miyake, or Madame Grès are real artists to me. Designing a combat system and building a garment is the same obsession.

For 2027, I'm looking for a Game Programmer/Designer position in games, ideally in Asia or a Nordic country, but genuinely open to anywhere in the world. If you're working on something ambitious, get in touch.
