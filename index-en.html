Journey

Welcome to my portfolio!

My name is Louis, and I’m a Franco-Finnish developer, freshly graduated with a Master’s in Game Technology from BUAS (Breda University of Applied Sciences). Right now, I’m travelling, gaming, and coding. Well, not necessarily in that order. Travelling is a real passion of mine: seeing different cultures and how other people live, play, and create changes the way you look at just about everything.

On the coding part, I’m working on a fast-paced arena shooter inspired by Quake and Team Fortress 2, developed in Unreal Engine 5. It’s a way for me to get to grips with the engine, but more importantly, to make a game that I actually want to play. I also have over 150h of experience with GameMaker through Hyper Pulsar, a hardcore top-down shooter inspired by Hotline Miami and Katana Zero, which I developed solo during my gap year. I’m also a big fan of soulslikes, which happens to be closely related to the subject of my Master’s thesis.

Now, what really interests me is game feel. That thing players can feel without necessarily knowing exactly why, that attention to invisible details that can change everything. And that connects to my third passion: haute couture & fashion design. Designers like Yohji Yamamoto, Issey Miyake, Madame Grès, and Carol Christian Poell are true artists in my eyes. Designing a combat system and constructing a garment come from the same obsession and creativity.

For 2027, I’m looking for a position as a Game Programmer/Designer, ideally in Asia or a Nordic country, but honestly, I’m open to anywhere in the world. If you’re working on something ambitious, let's get in touch.
