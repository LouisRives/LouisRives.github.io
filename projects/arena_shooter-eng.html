Arena Shooter UE5

◆  FAST-PACED  ·  ARENA  ·  UNREAL ENGINE 5  ◆

✦

A fast-paced 1v1 arena shooter built in Unreal Engine 5. The concept is straightforward: two players, a small map, instant respawns, and a game that punishes hesitation. Potentially scaling to 2v2 if time and resources allow. Directly inspired by Quake and the TF2 MGE community mod, that specific kind of competitive format where every match comes down to skill, nothing else.

The main priority right now is movement and controls. Before anything else gets added, the game needs to feel right. That means the physics, the camera, the hit feedback and the audio all have to work together. A lot of small invisible tricks go into making that happen: hitstops, frame-precise animations, visual and audio cues timed to the action. The kind of detail that's completely unnoticeable when it's working, and immediately wrong when it isn't. Movement mechanics implemented so far:

  • Ground Pound + boosted jump & slide
  • Dash
  • Slide
  • Wall Jump + Wall Run
  • Rocket Launcher & Rocket Jumping
  • Air Strafing
  • Bhop + input buffer
  • Surf
  • Ramp Sliding
  • Smooth Camera

The end goal is two playable classes: Rocket Launcher + Shotgun and Sniper + Pistol. The design philosophy is easy to pick up, very hard to master. Rocket jumps, wall runs, surf ramps and bhop chains should reward players who put the hours in, while keeping the game readable and approachable for newcomers. Fast by design: instant respawn, small maps with few covers, rollout routes that reward mechanical knowledge.
